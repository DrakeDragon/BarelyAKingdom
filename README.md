# Barely a Kingdom

**A funny, addictive LitRPG kingdom builder that runs in a single HTML file.**

You start as a ditch cleaner in a swamp village with seven subjects, one goose, and a talking crown named Hubert. You end up ruling everything, mostly by accident. Grow your hero from zero to hero while your tiny puddle of a kingdom grows into an empire, and survive the Great Audit.

## Features

### Story
- 15 chapters across 5 acts: The Puddle, Growing Pains, The Paper Trail, The Crown's Secret, The Great Audit
- Cutscenes with talking portraits, mood-based expressions, backgrounds and typewriter dialogue
- Choices with stat checks and visible odds that change how the story ends
- 3 endings (gold, tin, or your own)
- Replay any finished chapter from the Chronicle

### Boss fights
- 5 turn-based bosses with telegraphed next moves, two-phase final boss
- Strike, Outwit, Inspire and Brace, plus a special ability from each friend
- Determination bonus after a loss, so nobody gets stuck

### Kingdom management
- 8 ranks, from Puddle With a Flag to Cosmic Monarchy
- 30 buildings that unlock as you rank up
- Resources: coin, grub, lumber, subjects and vibe (starve them or bore them and things go wrong)
- A canvas diorama of your realm with a day/night cycle, walking subjects, weather and a keep that evolves with rank
- Petitions: short comedy decisions with skill checks

### Hero progression
- Five stats (Might, Wits, Charm, Grit, Luck), levels, titles and stat points
- Procedural loot with rarities, equipment slots and a bag
- 5 regions of quests with 3-star mastery
- 31 achievements and a running to-do goal list

### The Royal Court
- 7 companions who join through the story, each with a bond track and passive bonus
- 16 Royal Edicts bought with Royal Seals, in four branches
- Royal Actions with cooldowns, a Decree Frenzy meter, Golden Goose events, a 7-day daily gift streak
- Offline progress, and Abdication (prestige) that starts a new dynasty with permanent Legacy bonuses
